LOCAL TEXT MODEL

Transcript cleanup

A second, text-only model that fixes grammar and punctuation after speech recognition. It runs on this machine, never sees your audio, and keeps the standard transcript if cleanup times out or fails its checks. Corrections can still make mistakes.
